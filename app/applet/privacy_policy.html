Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you use the Merge Number mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to minimize data collection. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

  • Local Game Data: Your game progress, high scores, unlocked levels, and available hints are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Usage Data: We may collect non-identifiable usage data (such as crash logs or performance metrics) to help us improve the game experience.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

We use third-party advertising companies, specifically Google AdMob, to serve advertisements when you visit the App (including Rewarded and Interstitial ads). These companies may use information (not including your name, address, email address, or telephone number) about your visits to this and other apps in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:
How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps

3. Device Permissions

The App requires the following device permissions to function correctly:

  • Internet Access: Required to request and display advertisements.
  • Network State: Required to check if an active internet connection is available before requesting ads.

4. Data Security

Your game progress is stored securely on your local device's internal storage using Android's built-in Room Database. We do not transmit or store your personal game data on any remote cloud servers.

5. Children's Privacy

This App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this Privacy Policy.
